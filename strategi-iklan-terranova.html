Struktur Campaign (TOFU → MOFU → BOFU)

TOFU — Awareness
Kenalkan Terranova ke Audiens Baru
  • Objective: Reach / Video Views
  • Budget: 25% total budget
  • Format: Video pendek 15-30 detik
  • Konten: Fasad, area perumahan, lokasi
  • Audience: Cold — interest + location
  • Duration: Tayang terus sebagai brand awareness
MOFU — Consideration
Edukasi dan Tumbuhkan Minat
  • Objective: Lead Generation / Traffic
  • Budget: 50% total budget
  • Format: Carousel + Single Image + Video
  • Konten: Interior, denah, harga, KPR info
  • Audience: Video viewer 25%+ dari TOFU
  • CTA: "Tanya Harga", "Download Brosur"
BOFU — Conversion
Dorong Booking & Survey
  • Objective: Messages / Conversion
  • Budget: 25% total budget
  • Format: Single Image + Lead Form
  • Konten: Promo, urgency, FOMO, testimonial
  • Audience: Website visitors, lead form viewers
  • CTA: "Booking Sekarang", "Jadwal Survey"

📐 Struktur Ad Set yang Direkomendasikan

Campaign 1: Awareness — Video Views
Ad Set A: Interest – Properti Bandung
Ad Set B: Location – 35km dari pin
Campaign 2: Lead Generation — KPR Info
Ad Set A: Lookalike 1% dari buyer list
Ad Set B: Video viewer 50%+ retargeting
Campaign 3: Conversion — Booking
Ad Set A: Website pixel retargeting
Ad Set B: Lead form opener (belum konversi)

⚙️ Pengaturan Teknis Wajib

✅ Meta Pixel — pasang di semua halaman landing page
✅ Conversion API (CAPI) — backup data pixel jika iOS block
✅ Lead Form Native — form di dalam FB/IG untuk mobile-first
✅ WhatsApp Business API — click-to-WhatsApp ads
✅ Catalog Properti — listing tiap unit jadi Dynamic Ads
✅ Custom Events: ViewContent, Lead, InitiateCheckout
✅ UTM Parameter — tracking per campaign/ad set/ad
⚡ Advantage+ Campaign — AI optimization Meta terbaru
💡 Tips Advanced: Gunakan Advantage+ Audience di MOFU campaign — biarkan Meta AI mencari audience sendiri berdasarkan sinyal pixel. Terbukti menurunkan CPL 20–40% dibanding manual targeting untuk property leads di Indonesia.

Strategi Lead Generation & Conversion Funnel

Dari Klik Iklan Jadi Booking

📊 Conversion Funnel Terranova

👁️ Tayangan Iklan (Impressions) 100.000+/bln
↓ ~3% klik
🖱️ Klik ke Landing Page 3.000–5.000
↓ ~5% isi form
📋 Submit Form / Chat WA 150–250 leads
↓ 40% leads qualified
✅ Leads Qualified (HOT) 60–100 hot leads
↓ 30% setuju survey
📅 Survey ke Lokasi 18–30 orang
↓ 20–30% booking
🏠 BOOKING UNIT! 3–9 unit/bln

*Proyeksi dengan budget iklan Rp 15 Juta/bulan (paket Growth)

⚙️ Lead Management SOP

⚡ Respon Maksimal 5 Menit
Setiap lead masuk WAJIB direspon <5 menit. Lead properti yang direspon dalam 5 menit memiliki konversi 9× lebih tinggi. Setup WA Business auto-reply.
📱 Lead Qualification Script
Tanyakan: Budget? Cash/KPR? Timeline beli? Untuk siapa? Ini membantu filter HOT vs COLD lead agar tim sales fokus ke yang berpotensi closing.
📆 Follow-up Jadwal
Hari 1: Respon + kirim brosur digital. Hari 3: Follow-up jadwal survey. Hari 7: Reminder promo. Hari 14: Final offer. Gunakan CRM sederhana (Google Sheet atau Notion).
🔁 Retargeting Cold Leads
Lead yang belum konversi di-retarget ulang via Meta Ads (upload list ke Custom Audience) dengan materi iklan berbeda: testimonial, urgency stok, atau promo baru.
📊 Tools Rekomendasi
CRM: Google Sheet CRM template (gratis) atau HubSpot Free. WA Blast: WhatsApp Business API. Laporan mingguan otomatis dari Meta Ads Manager.
